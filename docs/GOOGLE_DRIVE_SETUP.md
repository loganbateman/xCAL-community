# Connect your team

Install [xCAL Community](https://github.com/loganbateman/xCAL-community/releases), open it and sign in with your Google account. No configuration files are needed.

## Start a team

1. Choose **Create a Team** after signing in.
2. Enter a team name and choose or create a dedicated Google Drive folder.
3. Confirm to open the team workspace.

For this beta, use a separate test folder.

## Invite someone

1. Open **Settings > Team**.
2. Enter their Google account email and choose **Invite teammate**.
3. They accept Google's sharing email, open Community and sign in with that account.
4. They choose your team from the team list. Use **Refresh teams** if it is missing.

There is no join code. Google Drive sharing controls access. Editors can invite people when the owner allows editors to manage sharing.

## Already in a team?

Sign in and choose your team. Community remembers your account. Use **Settings > Team > Switch team** to open a different team.

The team folder link in Settings opens your shared files in Google Drive.

## If something goes wrong

- **Team missing:** confirm the invitation was accepted with the same Google account, then refresh the team list.
- **Sign-in blocked:** contact the maintainer with the exact Google message. You do not need to create a Google Cloud project.
- **Changes waiting:** check your connection and use **Retry Now** in the Sync Queue.
- **Before switching teams:** sync pending edits. Backups are in **Settings > Workspace > Backups**.

Leaving a team disconnects it on this device; it does not remove your Google Drive access.
